Leafrest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Developer: Indilab Project

Leafrest gives you a daily deck of 21 cards (checked classic quotes, science facts, myths and quizzes), then the feed ends. It works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what that means for your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Leafrest has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Your settings, saved cards and stats

Your language, topics, theme, saved cards, quiz answers, streak and reading stats are stored only inside the app on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling Leafrest deletes all of it.

When you share a card image or export your saved cards, the file goes only where you choose to send it, using your phone's share sheet.

Purchases

Leafrest Pro is a one-time purchase handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether Pro has been purchased on your store account. The store's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Permissions

  • Notifications – one daily reminder at the time you choose, scheduled on your phone. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Internet – used only by the App Store / Google Play for the Pro purchase, and when you tap Read the full text to open a public-domain source (Project Gutenberg) in your browser. All cards and sounds are inside the app.

Children

Leafrest is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Support

Questions, a wrong quote, or ideas? Email indilabp@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Lost Pro after reinstalling or changing phones? Open Settings → Leafrest Pro and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same store account.

Kebijakan Privasi (Bahasa Indonesia)

Leafrest tidak mengumpulkan data apa pun. Tanpa akun, tanpa iklan, tanpa analitik, tanpa pelacak. Bahasa, topik, tema, kartu tersimpan, jawaban kuis, rentetan dan statistik hanya tersimpan di HP-mu dan terhapus bila app dihapus.

Pembelian Pro diproses App Store atau Google Play; kami tidak pernah melihat data pembayaranmu. Notifikasi hanya satu pengingat harian di jam pilihanmu dan bisa dimatikan kapan saja.
